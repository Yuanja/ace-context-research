# Addressable Recall Compaction

ARC makes archived observations explicitly addressable. Rather than hoping a summary or vector retriever preserves a detail, the system leaves a dereferenceable citation in the active transcript.

This is especially relevant for tool-heavy agents, where exact prior observations, command outputs, and error messages may matter later.
