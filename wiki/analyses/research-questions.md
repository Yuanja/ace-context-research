# Critical Research Questions

## Evaluation

- Are the benchmarks representative of real agent work, or mostly synthetic/search/coding tasks?
- Do gains persist across models, domains, longer horizons, and contradictory evidence?
- Are learned playbooks generalizable or benchmark-contaminated?

## Scale

- What happens with thousands of memory objects across multi-day, multi-project tasks?
- Does the cost of reflection, curation, storage, and recall still beat simpler summaries?
- How does context governance work across multiple users or teams?

## Monitoring

- Which metrics catch context drift, stale recall, missing citations, and harmful compression?
- Can alerts distinguish genuinely novel research from SEO commentary?
- How should bad context rules be retired?

## Safety

- Can poisoned tool observations become durable instructions?
- How are private or task-local facts prevented from becoming global playbook rules?
- Are context edits append-only, diffable, reversible, and source-attributable?
